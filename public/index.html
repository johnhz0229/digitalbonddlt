THE BUSINESS QUESTION

Can selected bond lifecycle rules be executed on a shared ledger?

The prototype explores how investor eligibility, ownership, coupon allocation and principal redemption can move from fragmented process steps into transparent, executable rules.

WHAT THIS PROTOTYPE SHOWS

One instrument. One state. Five controlled events.

01

02

03

PRODUCTION GAP ANALYSIS

What a regulated implementation still needs

The prototype demonstrates process logic, not production readiness.

IDENTITYKYC/AML integration

Binding legal identities, onboarding evidence and sanctions controls.

SETTLEMENTRegulated cash leg

Tokenized deposit, central-bank money or external payment-versus-delivery.

GOVERNANCEInstitutional controls

Role separation, multisignature approval, pause and recovery processes.

PRIVACYConfidentiality design

Protection of positions, investor data and commercially sensitive activity.

SCALEClaim-based payments

Record-date snapshots and scalable distribution instead of holder loops.

INTEGRATIONCapital-markets stack

Connections to treasury, custody, CSD, trading and reporting systems.

Prototype · Educational use only

Each browser receives temporary isolated state. The prototype is not audited, legally binding or production-ready, and uses no real money, securities or investor data.
